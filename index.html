Coupon App
Any Coupon for Any Shop
B2C SaaS Mobile

Case Study Summary

ProblemThe reluctance of small businesses to accept coupons from major vendors complicates HR's ability to provide small gestures to employees for routine employee events (ex: new employee, newborn baby, friend referral, etc).

SolutionA branded app pre-loaded by HR with a set value, usable at any business with ready-made templates for various events.

ResultEmployee morale was boosted by the branded app, and motivation rose with the expectation of small gifts. In addition, users could spend their coupon credit as they wished.

Secure Connect App
Secure Remote Work Environment
B2C SaaS Mobile ZTNA IT & OT Cyber Security

Case Study Summary

ProblemCompany employees working remotely in the field and using mobile devices needed to access their company network resources.

SolutionZTNA Agent app that allows users to gain access to network-based resources. This app utilizes a VPN service to offer a secure connection while ensuring no user data is shared with third parties.

ResultUsers enjoy a simplified and supervised app that allows them to connect to their environment with a single button after entering their account domain name and the credentials received from their Administrator.
